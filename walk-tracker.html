Import from Apple Health

Export your Apple Health data on your iPhone, then import the export.xml file here. Only walking workouts are imported — all other data is ignored.

How to export from Apple Health →
  1. Open the Health app on your iPhone
  2. Tap your profile picture (top right)
  3. Tap Export All Health Data
  4. Tap Export and wait (may take a minute)
  5. Share the ZIP file to your Mac via AirDrop or Files
  6. Unzip it — find apple_health_export/export.xml
  7. Drop that file into the box below
