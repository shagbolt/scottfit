Shagbolt

Shagbolt is an early English variant of sackbut — the Renaissance ancestor of the trombone. It seemed like a good name for a site about music, words, and whatever else catches my attention.

I'm Scott Mousseau: bass trombonist, paralegal, reader, and resident of Roanoke, Virginia. Poke around.
